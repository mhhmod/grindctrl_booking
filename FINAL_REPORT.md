## What changed

The rejected renderer cached SVG screenshots by visual-state key, leaving repeated fixed-window compositions and weak movement. V2 uses actual Remotion frame rendering, 12 major sequences per film, native editable React/CSS UI, measured type/search/copy/lookup/note/upload/reply states, intentional camera changes and different employee/retail compositions. The story remains employee-first; systems support the experience. The new source lets a designer change typography, fields, timing and motion without rewriting a rendering engine.

New fictional navy-linen photography was generated as a consistent material-led product family. The worn portrait was conditioned on the selected garment; the before portrait was edited from that same result. The result uses a face-preserving comparison reveal, not a distorted crop or a claim of exact body measurements. The Store Chat example now provides a useful merchant-fixture-grounded answer and retains context through a reasoned human handoff.

Compare the actual encoded narrative moments in [Film01 before/after](reviews/final/01-BEFORE-AFTER.jpg), [Film02 before/after](reviews/final/02-BEFORE-AFTER.jpg) and [Film03 before/after](reviews/final/03-BEFORE-AFTER.jpg). Separate final contact sheets show every major scene. These are concrete review evidence, not a perfect-score creative claim.

## Scope and research

Official Genesys Client Apps SDK and Conversations API guidance were accessible and inspected; vendor help pages and Concrete live screens were not available. Official Remotion/Motion/GSAP/Rive/R3F/Blender references were inspected through accessible repositories where public sites were blocked. Authorized local GRINDCTRL source and supplied proofs informed branding and product patterns. These evidence classes are kept separate. No public-site access or installed tenant feature is fabricated.

The Concrete films propose a new collaboration. Manual Zoho Desk phone search is user-reported. The 2023 contracted CRM URL-pop scope requires inspection before choosing new infrastructure. Phone matching is not identity verification. Membership source, note storage, individual call association, retention, author preservation and permissions remain discovery items. Human call notes are distinct from ticket notes and AI summaries. The fashion film uses a fictional merchant and authored illustrations; conditional order lookup is read-only and requires configuration/verification.
