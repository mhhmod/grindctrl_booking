# Asset and narration provenance

The three attached new ElevenLabs MP3s are the sole narration sources. Their exact upload-name mapping, durations and hashes are in AUDIO_SOURCE_INVENTORY.json. No narrator, voice/model ID or account settings were invented; metadata identifying them was not exposed. No old rejected recording was reused, no narration regenerated, and no speed/pitch changes applied.

New navy-linen photography was generated for this project as a synthetic adult/garment asset sheet. A second image-tool pass corrected the upper-right pose and unrolled sleeves to preserve person/garment continuity. The delivered image is an authored illustration, not a real customer photograph or service-generated try-on result. UI text is native vector text, not baked into generated photographs.

GRINDCTRL/Genesys/Zoho marks and Manrope font were supplied in existing project assets. Manrope's OFL notice is bundled. Concrete is a plain typeset name because a verified official logo was unavailable.

The restrained ambient/piano score and very selective UI taps are newly synthesized by source/rebuild-audio.py. No external track, famous song, purchased sample, subscription or extra spoken voice was used. The code/score is supplied for these project films; no exclusive ownership of generic chord progressions is asserted.

All customer/agent names, dates, interactions, orders, notes and contact details are fictional. example.com is reserved example data and phone digits are masked. No private contracts, fees, signatures, credentials or real customer records are included.
