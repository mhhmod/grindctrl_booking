Implementation notes

All five concepts use one shared system: a 56px button, an 84px outer field (1.5× the button), and an 88px fixed placement box. The button sits at bottom: 16px and logical end: 16px; both its centre and the outer field's centre resolve to 44px 44px, leaving 2px of field clearance against each viewport edge.
