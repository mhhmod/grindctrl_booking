## Product logic and approval boundaries

- **Stylist:** eligible permitted catalogue items -> occasion/preferences -> coordinated candidate outfit -> customer refinement. Appointment remains conditional on a supported service; no booking created.
- **E-commerce:** query -> product options -> source-based size guide -> current approved stock. No stock source means unknown availability, not an invented result or guaranteed fit.
- **Copilot:** human agent and original human call note remain visible. Proposed AI guidance references a fictional policy excerpt; an approved policy source is required in production. AI-generated summary is explicitly DRAFT, separate from the original human note; employee edit/approval precede use/save. No ticket or financial decision is executed.
- **Clienteling:** approved customer use/consent -> source-verified membership/preferences/purchases -> permitted product suggestions. Membership is not inferred from behavior or phone number. Perks require current programme validation.
- **Operations:** same synthetic SKU/size at Branch A and candidate source at Branch B -> proposed reallocation from B to A -> accountable manager review/approve/reject. No inventory transfer, procurement or fulfilment executed. Source freshness, role permissions and forecasting backtests required.
- **Marketing:** brief -> three creative directions/sample copy -> human brand/editorial and rights review -> publication only after approval. Supplied illustrative imagery is not a production licence clearance or approved collection.
- **Executive:** permitted sources -> quality review -> concise illustrative question/insight -> visible original record references. No data connection or measured business KPI claimed.
- **Roadmap:** inspect actual systems/native capabilities -> prioritize value/feasibility -> scoped authorized pilot -> measure before expansion. No promise to deploy every opportunity at once.

Actual sources inspected and blocked/unusable endpoints are recorded in `research/access.json`. No credentials or confidential contract scans are distributed. This report distinguishes documented capabilities from operational proof.
