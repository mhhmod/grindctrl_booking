This note covers the merchant dashboard on grindctrl.cloud. The boards follow the journey in order: 1 Tell us about your store (Onboarding), 2 Connect your store (Connect), 3 Today while setting up (Today-Setup), 4 Today live (Today), then Inbox, Knowledge, ChatStudio, TryOnInsights, TryOnStudio, Plan, Command, Owner-Phone, Today-States and Team. Each has an Arabic twin, such as Today-AR, which replaces the retired Today-Arabic. Their screenshots are in `design/screens/dashboard`, at 1440 pixels in `desktop` and at 390 pixels in `phone`, with the twins in `design/screens/dashboard/ar`, plus the states after an interaction: Knowledge after approving, both studios with each preview state, Plan while confirming and after buying, and Command after applying its action. `07-board-map.md` maps each board to today's routes and components and lists every data source, and `09-backend-contracts.md` gives the contract for every element the boards outline as new. This note is about structure, behaviour and the cases the boards don't show.

The dashboard has one job for each person. The owner opens it, sees in ten seconds whether anything needs her, acts on it in one click, and leaves. Everything she needs to set up, check or change is one level down, with a live preview beside it. A team member opens it to answer shoppers and keep the assistant's answers right. The roles are today's Owner, Admin and Member, and the Team board states the rule: "Owners see everything, including billing. Admins manage settings. Members answer chats and keep answers up to date." A member sees only what helps with that and never sees billing. Alerts go by email today, and to their own phone during their shift once shifts and WhatsApp exist (items 13 and 14).

The information architecture replaces today's three items (`lib/dashboard/nav-config.ts`). The sidebar has, in order: the store switcher at the top, then Today, Inbox with the count of shoppers waiting, Try-On, Store Chat, Plan and Settings, then the looks meter and the signed-in person at the bottom. Try-On has two tabs, Results and Set up, and so does Store Chat, with What it knows and Set up. Settings has Team, Store details and Notifications. A team member's sidebar, shown on Inbox, has Today, Inbox, Try-On, Store Chat and My alerts. There is no Plan, Settings becomes their own alert settings, and the footer shows their name and shift ("Team, on shift until 10 pm"). Use routes that read like the nav. `/dashboard` is Today, `/dashboard/inbox` is the inbox with a conversation id for the open thread, `/dashboard/try-on` and `/dashboard/try-on/setup` are the Try-On tabs, `/dashboard/store-chat` and `/dashboard/store-chat/setup` are the Store Chat tabs, `/dashboard/plan` is Plan, and `/dashboard/settings` with one segment per tab is Settings. Redirect `/dashboard/overview` and `/dashboard/messenger` to the new homes so old links keep working. Delete the retired preview routes `07-board-map.md` names in the same change, so the new nav has no ghosts. Hide Plan and every billing action from admins and members on the server too, not only in the nav (phase zero item 12). The active store is resolved once on the server and shared by every page (item 33).
