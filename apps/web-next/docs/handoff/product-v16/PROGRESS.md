The job reports its stage while it is made (`received`, then `making` while the provider fits and renders the piece, then `finishing` while the look is saved), on `GET /api/try-on/jobs/[jobId]` as before, and the phone polls for up to 5 minutes, the provider's own worst case, instead of 2. Every outcome lands on the job by itself: a failed look is stored before its refund so a poll always ends even if the refund throws, and anything unexpected in the background ends a look still shown as being made as failed, in the shopper's words. The sheet's steps follow the real stage: the first step until the provider starts, the others paced by the store's estimate while it works, the last at once when the look is being saved (polls come every few seconds, so the short saving stage alone could not be relied on). Without the flag, nothing changes: the request waits for the look as it always has. No SQL.
