What of the product-v16 handoff is committed

The notes (README.md, 01-system.md to 09-backend-contracts.md, CLAUDE_CODE_PROMPT.md, PROGRESS.md), the copy files with every string in English and Arabic, tokens.json, the icon set and the asset manifest are committed here so code, tests and pull requests can point at them, and so PROGRESS.md travels with the work. Two parts of the handoff bundle stay with the owner and are not committed, the same as for site-v15: `design/` (the screenshots of all 74 boards and their states, and the prototype's boards and tooling, about 19 MB) and `assets/images` (the sample store's photos, which never ship). Where notes or code comments name `design/screens/...` or `design/prototype/...`, they mean that folder in the owner's handoff bundle.
