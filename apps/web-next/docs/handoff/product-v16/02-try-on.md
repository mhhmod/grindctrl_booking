This note covers the shopper try-on on the merchant's store, board by board, and how it should behave beyond what a still board can show. The boards are TryOn-PDP, TryOn-Photo, TryOn-Check, TryOn-Waiting, TryOn-Result, TryOn-Browse, TryOn-Desktop, TryOn-States, TryOn-More and Studio-Phone. Each has an Arabic twin with the same name plus `-AR`. Their screenshots are in `design/screens/try-on`, numbered in journey order, with the twins in `design/screens/try-on/ar`, and the result board also has shots after switching to Your photo, after picking another colour and after adding to cart. `07-board-map.md` says which file each board replaces and what data it needs. `06-phase-zero.md` holds the backend items the journey depends on. The ones that matter most here are item 1 (hide when unavailable), item 2 (the photo promise), item 16 (asynchronous looks), item 17 (failure kinds), item 18 (product photos, pause and Not right), item 19 (colour, size and add to cart), item 20 (per-shopper limits), item 26 (sharing) and item 27 (the model path). With the Review switch on, the boards outline what needs new back end work: the time estimates, the promise line, the on-phone photo check, the busy queue that starts by itself, the model like you, Not right?, colour switching, sending the look itself, Ask about fit, Try these on you, the share sheet, the friend's look page and "Send to my phone". `09-backend-contracts.md` gives each one its contract and flag.

The journey in one breath. A shopper sees Try it on next to Add to cart, taps it, adds one photo, sees it checked on the phone, agrees once, keeps browsing while the look is made, and gets a look she can compare with her photo. She switches colour and size on the look itself, adds the exact variant to her cart without leaving, and tries other pieces with one tap each using the same photo. From then on, every product card in the store can show her look. She can save or send a look, ask the store about the fit, and come back to her looks for 24 hours. Every step shows the merchant's colours, font and corners, never GrindCTRL's. Every step is in Arabic when the store page is. Nothing is promised that the back end does not do.

There are four ways in. The first is the Try it on button on a product page, drawn by the app block. The second is a "Try it on" pill on product cards on collection and search pages, drawn by the app embed. The third is Try it on on a product card inside Store Chat, and "See it on me" when the assistant offers it (`03-store-chat.md`). The fourth is a try-on link that opens a product page with the sheet already open on the chosen variant. That link is what "Copy a try-on link" on TryOnInsights makes for Instagram bios and stories, and it is also what "Open in my browser" carries out of an in-app browser. Use one query parameter on the product URL for it, read by the block on load, and keep the variant in the link.

The product page block, TryOn-PDP. The button reads "Try it on" with a person icon. It is the GrindCTRL Try-On block, so its place is wherever the merchant puts the block in the theme editor, and the board shows it above Add to cart. On the board it matches the theme's Add to cart button in font, corner radius and colours, as an outline. Today the merchant sets the button colour, the text colour and the corners on TryOnStudio or Shopify-TryOn, and matching the theme and choosing between outline, filled and a text link are new. A quiet line under Add to cart repeats the promise: "Ready in about 20 seconds. Your photo is deleted after each look." The line is new. The seconds come from the store's own median time per look over its recent looks. When there are too few looks to be honest, the line drops the first sentence. The merchant can turn the whole line off ("Show the promise under the button"). The "Questions about this abaya? Ask us" row under it belongs to Store Chat. The button must not appear and then pop in. Phase zero item 1 ships first with the block hidden until config says the store is live, which avoids a flash but still shifts the page when the button appears. The v16 block removes the shift by deciding before the page paints. Keep the store's try-on state in an app-data metafield, and the per-product pause in a product metafield in the app's reserved namespace, both updated by our server whenever the state changes, so Liquid renders the block only for a live store and a live product. Liquid cannot see the visitor's 50/50 bucket or the owner's preview token, which live in first-party storage, so a few lines of inline script inside the block read them while the page is parsed and hide the button for the holdout arm, or show it to a preview-token holder, before the first paint. The config call confirms afterwards, and a mismatch hides the button. Check Shopify's current documentation for the exact Liquid access path to app-owned metafields. The colour and size pickers are the theme's own. The variant the shopper has selected travels with the tap (item 19).

The try-on opens as a bottom sheet on phones and as a centred dialog on desktop, and its contents are our iframe. The sheet rises to about 90 percent of the screen, leaves the store dimmed above it, and has a grab handle and a 44 pixel close button. It closes on the close button, on a swipe down, on a tap on the dimmed store, on Escape and on the phone's back gesture (push a history entry when it opens). The store behind it does not scroll. Focus moves into the sheet when it opens and returns to the Try it on button when it closes. Remember the photo, the consent and the chosen variant for the visit. Closing the sheet never throws a look away. The iframe needs `allow="camera"` for Take a photo, which it lacks today.

The photo step, TryOn-Photo. The header shows the piece with its colour and size ("Layla Embroidered Abaya, Black · M"), so the shopper knows what she is trying on. The title is "See it on you", with one sentence: "Add one photo where you stand straight, head to feet. It takes about 20 seconds." The seconds again come from the measured median. Three small tiles coach the photo: head to feet in frame, hijab and loose clothes are fine, good light and a plain wall. Then come three choices. "Take a photo" opens the camera inside the sheet, with a body outline and short prompts while she stands back. Offer a three second timer so she can step away, and a switch between the front and back cameras. "Choose from your photos" opens the gallery and must accept iPhone HEIC photos, converting on the phone or on our server (item 29). "No photo? Use a model like you" leads to the model path from TryOn-More and only appears once the owner has decided where model images come from (item 27). At the bottom, a lock line says "Your photo is used only to make your look, then deleted from our service." with a link, "How photos are handled", which opens the privacy notice inside the sheet in the shopper's language. If the shopper kept her photo from an earlier look, skip this step and go straight to the check, with "Use a different photo" there.

The check and consent, TryOn-Check. The photo is checked on the phone before anything is sent. The checks are head to feet, one person, bright and sharp, and standing straight, each with a tick, under a green "Looks great". The check is a small on-device model or a set of simple rules (item 17). It must run in our iframe and take well under a second on a mid-range phone. A failing check changes the screen to the first phone of TryOn-States. It names the fix in plain words ("Step back a little so your feet show"), marks the failing line, explains why it matters for this piece ("Full length helps us get the hem and the fall of the abaya right."), says "Nothing has been sent yet.", and offers "Take it again" and "Use this photo anyway". The first time only, a consent block follows. "Before your first try-on" says in two sentences that the photo goes to our secure AI service only to make this preview and that the service deletes it right after each look. "We only ask this once" means exactly that. Store the consent on the phone apart from the photo and looks, with its own expiry of about a year, so the promise stays true after the photo is gone. Delete all clears it, and the owner signs off the period with the other retention periods (item 34). "Keep my photo on this phone" is on by default and says "Try other pieces in one tap. Kept on this phone for 24 hours." Turning it off means nothing is written to the phone's storage. The photo lives only in memory for this sheet, a look being made can still follow her to the next page through its job token, and the finished look is kept in memory only while that page is open. My looks then stays empty, and product cards do not show "Your look" after she leaves the page. The main button is "Agree and try it on" the first time and "Try it on" after that, with "By continuing you agree to the try-on privacy notice." under it. When the switch is on, the phone keeps the photo and the looks in IndexedDB in our iframe's origin, each with a 24 hour expiry, purged on every load (item 2). The on-phone photo check is new.

Making the look, TryOn-Waiting. The look is made asynchronously (item 16). The sheet shows the shopper's photo with the piece beside it, a thin progress bar, "Making your look" and "About 12 seconds left", then the store's loading steps. By default they are "Reading your photo", "Fitting the garment" and "Rendering your look", and a merchant can write their own or pick another loading animation on TryOnStudio. Today a job reports only processing, then completed or failed, so the steps cannot follow real server stages. Show them in order while the job runs, hold the last one until the result arrives, and switch to real stages once item 16 adds them. Done steps get a tick, the current one a spinner and the rest stay dim. The time estimate is new. It comes from the server's estimate and counts down smoothly. If the estimate runs out before the look is ready, keep the current step and drop the seconds line rather than showing zero or a negative number. "Keep browsing, I'll tell you when it's ready" and the chevron at the top both shrink the sheet into the floating chip from TryOn-Browse and let the shopper go anywhere in the store. Cancel stops the job. Phase zero item 16 says when that refunds the look. When the service is busy, the second phone of TryOn-States replaces the steps with a countdown ring, which is new: "Lots of people are trying on right now", "We will start yours automatically in 12 seconds. You keep your place and your photo.", the piece, "Keep browsing, I'll tell you" and Cancel. The queue starts the look by itself. Announce the step changes and the ready moment through a polite live region.

The look, TryOn-Result. The title is "Your look is ready" with the piece and price, and a close button. The look fills the top of the sheet. A Look and Your photo switch at its top corner compares the look with the original photo. "Change photo" shows on the Your photo view and starts again with a new photo, which is today's "Try with a different photo". An "AI preview" label stays on the look. "Not right?" sits in the bottom corner. Both labels hide while Your photo is showing, because they belong to the look. Under the look are the colour swatches with the colour's name, the sizes, and one big "Add to cart · Black, M" button whose label always names the variant it will add. Picking another colour makes a new look in that colour from the same photo. The look dims under "Making it in Sand" and "About 10 seconds, same photo", and every look made in this sheet is kept, so switching back is instant. Not right? and the colour switch are new. Whether each colour switch costs a look is an open question for the owner, and the README gives the default to build. Picking a size changes only what Add to cart adds, because a size does not change the picture. A sold-out variant shows as unavailable on its swatch or size and its button says "Sold out". Add to cart adds that exact variant through the loader's call to the store's own `/cart/add.js`. The sheet stays open and a small bar appears at its bottom: "Added Sand, M" with "Checkout". Ask the loader to refresh the theme's cart count, using the theme's cart events where they exist. Never redirect to `/cart` the way `tryon.js:221` does today.

Under the main button are three actions. Today the merchant's On the look switches decide which actions show, and they are Add to cart, Save photo, Send on WhatsApp, which opens WhatsApp with a short message about the piece, and Try with a different photo. On the board, Send, which sends the look itself, and Ask about fit are new. "Save photo" uses the phone's share sheet with the image file where the browser supports it, so "Save image" is one tap, and falls back to a download. The saved image carries the "AI preview" mark and the store's name, drawn into the image inside our iframe on the phone, so nothing extra is kept on our server. Share links reuse the same marked image (item 26). "Send" opens the "Send your look" sheet from TryOn-More. "Ask about fit" opens Store Chat with this look attached as the shopper's own message, which counts as sending the look to the store (item 26). Below them, "Try these on you, one tap each" shows other pieces as small cards, with "Uses this photo" on the right. Take them from the store's recommendations for this product, keep only pieces that can be tried on, and give each its own signed context. A tap starts a look for that piece with the same photo and comes back to this screen when it is ready. The row only appears when the shopper's photo is available, in memory or kept on the phone. The Try these row is new, and it is a sideways scroller inside its own track. Under the actions sits the merchant's note under each look, set on TryOnStudio, which on the board reads "This preview is a visual guide, not an exact sizing guarantee. Colours may vary slightly." The standard note shows when the merchant leaves it empty. On a short phone, 375 by 667, the sheet scrolls inside with its header pinned.

"Not right?" is how shoppers tell the merchant a look failed. It is also the input for the "said not right" rate on TryOnStudio, TryOnInsights, Today and Command (item 18). A tap records the flag on this look and opens the third phone of TryOn-States as a panel: "This look did not come out right", "This piece's photo makes it hard to show on you. Here are other ways to see how it fits.", "See it on a model like you" (only when the model path exists), "Try another piece" and "Ask about the fit". The same screen appears, without a flag from the shopper, when the service returns a failure caused by the product photo. Failures of other kinds get their own plain sentence from item 17, in the shopper's language. A failed look never uses one of the store's looks, and the shopper's photo stays so she can try again.

Collections, TryOn-Browse. On collection and search pages, each card for a piece that can be tried on gets a "Try it on" pill in the theme's style on the image's bottom corner. Pieces that are paused, have no usable photo or can't be worn (bags, scarves, gift cards) get no pill. A tap opens the same sheet for that product. A card the shopper has already tried shows "Your look" with a swap button that flips the card between the store's photo and her look. The card being made shows "Trying on" with a spinner. A floating chip at the bottom of the screen follows the shopper from page to page while a look is being made: her photo with a progress ring, "Making your Zahra look", "About 9 seconds. Keep browsing.", and "My looks". When the look is ready, the chip says "Your look is ready" and a tap opens it. The chip sits above the theme's sticky bars, and while it shows, the chat bubble lifts above it, so the two never overlap. Here is the rule that changes the build. Her photo and her looks must never be drawn into the store's page itself (item 2). So the "Your look" picture on a card is a small iframe from our origin laid exactly over the card image, and so is the chip. The loader positions them, and nothing in the store's DOM ever holds the image. The pill labels are translated, and Arabic product handles must work (item 29). The time estimate on the floating chip is new.

My looks, the fourth phone of TryOn-States. It opens from the chip or from the result. It is titled "My looks", says how many looks are kept on this phone, and shows each look with "AI preview", the piece, colour and size, "Add" and a WhatsApp button. A look still being made shows "Making it, about 6 seconds". The footer repeats the promise: "Your photo and looks stay on this phone for 24 hours. The store sees a look only if you send it." "Delete all" removes the photo and every look from the phone at once and closes the sheet. My looks covers this store only, because browsers keep our iframe's storage separately for each store. Private windows and storage clearing can empty it early, so treat an empty store as the normal case and simply leave the entry out.

Arabic. Every try-on board has an Arabic twin that shows the same screen right to left, and TryOn-Result-AR replaces the retired TryOn-Arabic board. Text aligns to the right, the compare switch and chips change sides, the swatches and sizes run right to left, and digits stay Western ("3,250 ج.م"). The sample store addresses women, so the actions are feminine, for example "غيّري الصورة", "احفظي", "أرسلي", "اسألي عن المقاس", "غير مناسبة؟" and "جربي هذه عليكِ بلمسة واحدة". The store's Arabic address setting picks feminine, masculine or neutral forms (`01-system.md`), and that setting is new. Every twin string is in `copy/try-on.json` with its status. Use the approved ones exactly, and list the drafts you ship and any masculine or neutral forms you write for the owner. Colour names come from the store's own variant names. The twins show the store's Arabic names ("أسود", "رملي", "زيتي"), which Shopify can supply through the store's translations.

Desktop, TryOn-Desktop. On desktop the try-on is a centred dialog over the product page, 1040 by 716 pixels on a 1440 by 900 screen, keeping a margin on smaller screens. The look side is a before and after slider between "Your photo" and "Your look". It has a round handle you can drag, move with the arrow keys or click to position, and it is labelled for screen readers as a comparison slider. "AI preview" and "Not right?" sit on the look side. The details side shows "Your look is ready, made in 14 seconds" from the job's real duration, the product and price, "Colour, Black" with "Pick a colour to see it on you", "Size, M" with "Size guide" (only when the store has a size chart for the piece, item 25), "Add to cart · Black, M", "Save image", "Send to my phone" and "Ask about the fit", then "Try these on you, one click each". "Send to my phone" shows a QR code for the look's share link, which needs no contact details. Sending to WhatsApp instead would need the opt-in from item 14. The photo and check steps use the same dialog, with the camera option hidden when the computer has no camera. The dialog fits a 1024 by 768 screen and scrolls inside. "Send to my phone" is new.

More ways in, TryOn-More. The first phone is the photo step inside Instagram's in-app browser, where the camera often cannot open. Detect in-app browsers from the user agent (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok). Show the note "You're in Instagram's browser, so the camera may not open here. A photo from your gallery works just as well.", naming the app the shopper is in, and make "Choose a photo" the main action. "Open in my browser" opens the same try-on link in the phone's own browser, carrying the piece, size and choices ("Your piece, size and choices come with you"). Android can do that with an intent link. iOS in-app browsers cannot be switched by a page, so on iOS copy the link and show one short sentence on how to open it from the app's menu. That sentence and the TikTok and Facebook versions of the note are new copy to draft and flag. The second phone is the model path, "See it on a model like you": height, usual size, with or without hijab, then a look of a model wearing the piece, labelled "Model, 160 cm, size M" and "AI preview", with Add to cart and "Use my own photo instead". It stays hidden until the owner decides where models come from (item 27). The third phone is the share sheet, "Send your look". It shows the image, the line "Layla Embroidered Abaya, Black, M. The image is marked AI preview and Maison Noor.", and WhatsApp, Story, Copy link and Save. A lock note says the link opens the look on the store's site for 7 days, only for people the shopper sends it to, and that she can delete it any time. At the bottom: "Maison Noor sees this look only if you send it to them". WhatsApp opens WhatsApp with the text and link and no fixed recipient. Story and Save use the share sheet with the image file. The fourth phone is the page a friend opens, at `maisonnoor.com/apps/grindctrl/look/8KQ2` through the App Proxy, rendered inside the store's theme. It shows the store name, the look with "AI preview", "A look made with Try it on", the piece with colour, size and price, "Try it on yourself" (the try-on link for that piece), "See the abaya" (the product page), and "Shared by the person who made it. This page disappears in 7 days." The page is excluded from search engines. After 7 days, or once the shopper deletes it, it shows the store's own not found page.

The website studio, Studio-Phone. This is GrindCTRL's own try-on page at `/try-on` on a phone. It already exists from v15, and GrindCTRL branding is right here. Keep everything v15 shipped and take four changes from the board. The photo card shows the on-device check result ("Looks great, head to feet"). The empty result says "Your look appears here" and "About 15 seconds after you tap Create", with the seconds measured. The footer line is "Your photo is used once for this preview, then deleted." And "Add it to your Shopify store" links to the App Store listing once it exists. Until the listing exists, leave the link out. The board was rebuilt so its top and bottom bars stay pinned while the middle scrolls. The photo check, the timing and the deletion promise on it are new.

When try-on is not available. The button and the pills disappear without a message when the store is out of looks, has not gone live, is paused, or the product is paused or has no usable photo (item 1 and item 18). The owner still sees everything through the preview token (item 9). "Test looks are free" refers to those preview looks, which item 9 covers. If a shopper is mid-flow when looks run out, or when she reaches the store's daily limit per shopper (item 20), the sheet ends the try-on politely and offers Ask about the fit. The boards don't show this, so draft the sentence and flag it. Never show a raw server message. Today the embed prints the server's English text even in Arabic (`try-on-demo.tsx:282`), which is exactly what must stop.

Events for item 22, sent from our iframe or the loader with the visitor id, bucket, product, variant and source. Send one when the button or a pill is seen, one when it is tapped, one when a photo is added (camera, gallery or model), one when a check fails (with the reason), one on consent, one when a look starts, one when it is ready (with milliseconds), one when it fails (with the kind), one on cancel, one when the sheet is minimised, one on Not right, one on a colour or size switch, one on add to cart (with the variant), one on save, one on share (with the channel), one on Ask about the fit, and one when a next piece is tapped. None of them carries the photo, the look or any text the shopper typed.

Try-on is done when a shopper on a 390 pixel phone and on a 1440 desktop, in English and Arabic, can go from the product page to a look and into her cart without leaving the page. The theme's own fonts and colours must show, with no GrindCTRL styling. The button must never appear when the store cannot make a look. The look must never appear in the store's DOM, which a test can check by scanning the page for our image URLs. Every number the shopper reads must come from measurement. Every state on the boards must be reachable in a test, either for real or with the job API mocked: check failed, busy, making, ready, colour switch, added, not right, failed, out of looks and My looks. Every try-on board and its twin must pass the responsive audit in `08-qa.md`.
