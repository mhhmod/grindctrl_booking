This note covers GrindCTRL inside Shopify admin. The boards are Shopify-Home-Setup, Shopify-Home, Shopify-TryOn, Shopify-Chat and Shopify-Plans, each with an Arabic twin such as Shopify-Home-AR, with screenshots in `design/screens/shopify-app`, at 1440 pixels in `desktop` and at 390 pixels in `phone`, the twins in `design/screens/shopify-app/ar`, plus the Try-On preview on its Look state and the Store Chat preview on its Team state. The boards draw Shopify's own top bar and admin navigation for context. Shopify renders those, and we render only the app area. `07-board-map.md` maps the boards to `apps/web-next/app/shopify/app` and `components/shopify`, and phase zero item 28 covers the frame, the connect flow and the Polaris question.

Many merchants will meet GrindCTRL here first, and some will never open grindctrl.cloud. So the admin app has to stand on its own for the daily basics: setting up, seeing that things work, seeing what needs attention, changing settings and managing the plan. Deep work such as Knowledge in full, results in full and the team stays on the web dashboard, while the Inbox has its own page in the app. The admin links to it in a new tab with the store already selected, and each such link carries the external link icon so nobody is surprised.

The frame. Use App Bridge's navigation menu for the app's pages. The app's name is GRINDCTRL in the admin nav and in the page headers, and every board shows the same navigation under it: Try-On, Store Chat, Inbox, Plans and Settings. The Settings link at the very bottom of the boards is Shopify's own. No board draws the app's Inbox or Settings page. Build the Inbox on the embedded conversations that exist today, laid out like the dashboard's Inbox, and ask the owner what the app's Settings page holds besides the linked account. Use App Bridge's title bar for page titles and the breadcrumb ("GRINDCTRL › Try-On"), and its contextual save bar on every editor. Drop what Shopify's design rules don't want inside admin: the GrindCTRL logo in the page body, the dark mode toggle, the "Claim this store" header button, and above all `AutoClaim`, which moves the merchant's top window out of admin without a click. Linking keeps today's direction. The dashboard's Connect page shows a code, and the merchant enters it in the app under "Link to dashboard". Once linked, the app shows "Linked to your GrindCTRL account" with a masked email and Change, as step 1 of Shopify-Home-Setup and the footer of Shopify-Home show (item 28). The pages are drawn in the Polaris look. If the owner confirms Polaris for the embedded admin, build them with Polaris web components (`s-page`, `s-section`, `s-banner`, `s-badge`, `s-button` and their siblings; check Shopify's current component list) and take every visual value from Polaris rather than from the boards. If the owner keeps Mantine, use a scoped Mantine theme that reproduces the Polaris look on the boards, and still use App Bridge's menu, title bar and save bar, which do not depend on Polaris. Either way, keep data loading, actions and copy in shared modules used by both the admin and the web dashboard, so the two never drift. One banner at a time, at the top of the page, is the rule.

The admin in Arabic. Every Shopify board has an Arabic twin, and Shopify-Home-AR replaces the retired Shopify-Arabic. When the admin's locale is Arabic, the app area turns right to left, every string is Arabic, digits stay Western ("16,800 ج.م", "18%"), and the App Bridge menu labels are rendered in Arabic by our app ("التجربة الافتراضية", "دردشة المتجر", "الرسائل", "الباقات", "الإعدادات"). The twins use neutral admin Arabic, close to Shopify's own. Every string is in `copy/shopify-app.json` with its status, so use the approved strings exactly and list the drafts you ship for the owner. Today the try-on half of the admin and the plan card are English only, so every string on the new pages needs its Arabic from that file.

On phones. Shopify admin's mobile app and narrow browsers show the app in a single column. Each `phone` screenshot shows how a page stacks: setup steps full width, the App embeds drawing under its step, tiles in two columns, the preview under the form, and the plans list under usage. Keep every action reachable without sideways scroll, and keep the save bar Shopify's own. Shopify controls stay at least 32 pixels tall, and the twins stack the same way, right to left.

The admin app is done when a merchant who installs from the App Store can finish setup without leaving Shopify admin, apart from the theme editor tab the steps send her to. Home must say truthfully what is on, what is broken and what needs her. Both settings pages must report every save truthfully through the save bar, Try-On going live within a minute and Store Chat through its draft and Publish, and must preview the result. Plans must show real usage and Shopify's real billing state. Every page must work in English and Arabic admin, match its twin, and pass the responsive audit in `08-qa.md`. Nothing may redirect the top window on its own. And the pages must pass Built for Shopify's checks, including the web performance thresholds for embedded apps.
