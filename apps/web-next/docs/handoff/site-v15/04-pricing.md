04, the pricing page

The pricing page shows the same plans, prices, packs and answers as the live page, drawn so the idea of a credit is seen rather than explained. One photo plus one piece makes one delivered look, and a generation that fails gives its credit back. The screenshots in `design/screens/pricing` show the whole page in all four layouts. The prototype is `design/prototype/generators/pricing11.py`, with `design/prototype/logic/pricing11.js`. In the prototype the plans and prices were typed in by hand. In production they come from the live catalog, as they do today.

Data stays where it is. `app/pricing/page.tsx` keeps its server work. It resolves the locale, resolves the currency from the `gc-currency` cookie, the geo lookup and then the browser language, and loads the catalog with `listPublicPlanCatalog()` and `plansForCurrency()`. It passes the result to the page content. The page wraps itself in `LandingLocaleProvider` with the golden root class, as the guard test requires. Everything the design shows per plan comes from a catalog row plus `components/pricing/pricing-copy.ts`: the plan name, the one line description, the price in the resolved currency formatted with the existing `formatCurrency`, the try-ons per month, the feature list, the recommended flag and the button. Don't hard code prices. `formatCurrency` and `formatNumber` are also imported by the ROI page, so keep their signatures.

Copy that is missing today. `getPlanCopyKey` in `pricing-page-content.tsx` only maps keys starting with `free-`, `launch-` and `dfy-`, and `pricing-copy.ts` only has entries for those. So Growth and Pro fall back to their English database names and descriptions, even on the Arabic page. Map the `growth-` and `pro-` prefixes and add their entries in both languages from `copy/pricing.json`. Growth becomes نمو with the description للمتاجر التي تجاوزت شهرها الأول., and Pro becomes احترافي with the description خطة النمو مع إعداد متكامل ننفّذه لك. Keep today's rule on which description wins: English shows the database description first, and Arabic shows the copy entry first. The Arabic button template `bookCallForPlan` gives احجز مكالمة عن نمو, but the design reads احجز مكالمة عن خطة نمو and احجز مكالمة عن الخطة الاحترافية. Add an optional per-plan button string to the plan copy entries, fall back to the template, and fill it from `copy/pricing.json` for Launch, Growth and Pro. The Free plan's Arabic description in the design matches the live Arabic page: اختبر تجربة المتجر المباشرة قبل الالتزام.

The header. On desktop, the shared floating header holds the GrindCTRL mark and name, a divider and a tag icon with the word Pricing. The middle holds three in-page tabs, each with its icon: Plans with a calendar, Top-ups with a stack and Questions with a chat bubble. The end holds the language switch, Sign in and Book a call. A tab scrolls smoothly to its section, instantly when motion is reduced, and each section has a 96px scroll margin at the top. On phones the header is the shared phone header, with the three sections in the menu as `01-system.md` describes.

The hero. A small chip with a tag icon reads AI Try-On pricing. The heading More shopper confidence, priced for real usage. is 50px on desktop and 32px on phones. The subtitle reads Start free, move up when try-on becomes part of your store. Then come Book a pricing call as the primary button, opening `BOOKING_URL` in a new tab, and Try the live demo as the secondary button, going to `/try-on`. On desktop the hero's end column holds the unit card. On phones it follows the buttons. The unit card is a figure of three image tiles joined by operators: a shopper photo marked Photo with a camera, a plus, the garment marked Piece with a hanger, an equals sign, and the finished look marked Look with a sparkle. Under them a caption reads 1 try-on, as an ink pill with the stack icon, then an equals sign, then 1 delivered image. The tiles are 118 by 153 on desktop and 92 by 120 on phones, with 16px rounded corners on the studio colour. The images are `shopper-woman.webp`, `garment-abaya.webp` (shown whole, inset 8px) and `woman-abaya.webp`. The whole figure is one image for assistive technology, with the label from `copy/pricing.json`.

The plans. A chip with a calendar icon reads Monthly plans, and the heading reads Pick the level of support your store needs. Put the existing currency toggle, Show prices in EGP or Show prices in USD, at the end of the heading row on desktop and under the heading on phones. Style it as a 44px outlined pill with the swap icon, and keep its logic exactly: set the cookie, then `router.refresh()`. The plans sit in one column below `lg`, two by two from `lg` to `xl` and in four equal columns with a 16px gap from `xl`, where each card is wide enough for a price like EGP 2,450 at 44px. Each card has 26px corners, the card colour, a 1px border and a soft shadow. Inside, from the top, come a 38px icon tile and the plan name at 19px. The icons are a sparkle for Free, a bolt for Launch, bars for Growth and blocks for Pro, picked by the plan key's prefix. Then the description, the price at 44px weight 700 followed by / month, and a try-ons line with the shirt icon and per month at the end. Under that a 6px meter shows the plan's try-ons against the largest plan on the page, never less than 3%. Then the feature list, where each feature has a 26px icon tile, and finally a full width 46px button. The recommended plan, the one whose key starts with `launch-`, gets a 1.5px ink border, a deeper shadow, an ink icon tile, a Recommended badge with a tick and the primary button style. The button rules stay as today. Free goes to `/sign-up`, and the paid plans open `BOOKING_URL` in a new tab. Every plan button is tracked as a plan button with its plan key, as `01-system.md` shows. Feature rows come from the copy for that plan, with the first always being Standard image quality. The design shows Done-for-you setup as a Pro feature because the Pro description says so. That is the managed setup claim, which needs the owner's approval first, as `01-system.md` explains. Until then, show only the features the copy lists today.

How credits work. Under the plans, one flat band explains a credit with a small diagram. It has a heading, How credits work, and a line, Plan credits are charged for delivered images. A dark node reads Credit reserved, When a shopper presses Generate, and forks into two light nodes. One reads Image delivered, The credit is used, and the other reads Generation failed, The credit comes back. Beside them sits a note with an info icon: Upstream provider cost may still be incurred on a failed generation. On desktop these are three columns: the heading at up to 250px, the diagram, then the note at up to 260px after a thin divider. On phones they stack, with a single downward arrow between the dark node and the two outcomes. The fork is an SVG that mirrors in Arabic. The band is one image for assistive technology, with the label from `copy/pricing.json`. The golden standard bans cards inside cards, so give the band a tinted background with no border, and let only the three nodes have borders.

Top-up packs. A chip with the stack icon reads Top-up packs, the heading reads Add credits without changing your plan., and the line under it reads Ask us to confirm current pack availability and terms before activation. On desktop the copy sits on the start side and a 600px list of packs on the end side. On phones they stack. The packs come from the catalog's credit packs. Each row has a 52px icon tile, the name (Boost 80 or Boost 75 Pro, kept in English), the number of renders with the photo icon, the price with one time under it, and a 44px round calendar button. That button opens `BOOKING_URL` and has no visible text, so its accessible name names the pack: Ask about Boost 80, or in Arabic اسأل عن Boost 80, as in `copy/pricing.json`. It is tracked as a pack button with the pack key. The premium pack gets an ink tile and a Premium badge with a sparkle.

Questions. A chip with the chat icon reads Questions, the heading reads Straight answers before you start., and the line under it reads Payment, activation, renewal, and contract terms are confirmed during booking. The questions and answers come from `pricing-copy.ts`, filtered by `lib/product-truth/public-register.ts` exactly as today, so only approved answers show. Today that is three: running out of credits, a failed generation and changing plans. The first answer says the try-on widget stops showing when credits run out. That isn't true in code yet, as `05-backend.md` explains, so raise it with the owner in the pull request rather than changing the approved copy yourself. Each question is a row with an icon tile and a plus that becomes a minus. The first is open on load and only one is open at a time. Use a real button with `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`, and keep the answer region in the page with `hidden` when it is closed, rather than removing it as the prototype did.

The closing band and footer. An ink band with rounded corners reads Want try-on to feel native to your store? and Book a short call. We will recommend the right plan and map the setup with you. It offers Book a pricing call as a cream button and Try the live demo as an outlined button, tracked with the `pricing_closing` placement. The footer is the shared footer.

Prototype pieces that don't ship. Drop the hand typed prices, the fixed 650 meter maximum, the CSS zoom, the width-based JS layout switch and the localStorage language key. The prototype's lone language button in the footer becomes the shared footer's language pair.
